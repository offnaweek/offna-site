About

OFF/NA grew out of a need for a space we were missing as young designers: a place where fashion can exist outside the institutional frameworks, not only as a product but as a cultural, social and creative event.

We started with a simple idea: to connect designers, makers and an audience, and to give independent work a stage through shows that feel less like the closed format of a “fashion week” and more like a live meeting of the local scene.

Instead of waiting to be invited into an existing system, we decided to build one of our own: more accessible, more experimental, and able to change together with the people who work within it.

Since then, every OFF/NA show has been an attempt to take that idea one step further: not just to show clothes, but to build a world around them, a community, and a moment people want to be part of.
